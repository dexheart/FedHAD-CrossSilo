# Individual figures for the revision

Auxiliary figures prepared during the revision; **none of them appears in the current
manuscript**, whose figures are mapped in `analysis/README.md`. Every artefact is generated by
`analysis/revision_consolidated_tables_figures/generate_individual_figures.py` from existing
results only, with the palette, marker mapping, typography and 300-dpi PDF/PNG export of
`analysis/main_campaign/figuras_artigo.py`. Each PDF has a same-named `.tex` figure float.

| Folder | Question covered | Figures |
|---|---|---|
| `01_efficiency` | accuracy against compute at the configured operating points (one point per method, not a frontier) and computation-to-target | four per-dataset plots and CIFAR-10 cost-to-target |
| `02_heterogeneity` | robustness under increasing skew, including FedNova | one plot per dataset |
| `03_long_horizon` | whether the severe-skew result survives a longer run | CIFAR-10 separately at alpha 0.5 and 0.01 |
| `04_ablation` | matched-budget and component controls | one forest plot per alpha |
| `05_drift_proxy` | relation of Hk to the raw update-alignment measure (which inherits the client-size effect of Section 6.4) and implemented control | one alignment plot per alpha plus control policy |
| `06_prior_assumption` | the uniform-global-prior limitation on FEMNIST | CV versus empirical-prior JS plot |

These figures predate part of the final evidence and do not include it: SCAFFOLD (Section 6.9),
the control batteries of Section 6.11 and the FEMNIST 50-round block. The `04_ablation` forest
plots show raw contrasts; the manuscript reports them with Holm adjustment (Table 15). Gaps that
remain in the manuscript: no long horizon at α = 0.1, no FedDyn, tuning for FedProx only, and no
instrumented runtime or energy measurement. The tables in `../tables/` retain the numeric detail
for FedNova coverage, partition verification and prior metrics.
